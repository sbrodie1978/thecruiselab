Pay table

Wild and scatter

The flask is wild. It stands in for every symbol except the ship's wheel, and pays on its own too.

The ship's wheel is the scatter. Three anywhere spins the bonus wheel. Four doubles the bonus. Five multiplies it by five.

The four bonuses

Happy Hour Bar. Pick glasses off the bar. Drinks add to your tab. Happy Hour raises the multiplier on every drink after it. Top Shelf doubles the tab. Beat the drinks package price and the whole tab doubles at closing. Two last orders bells and the bar closes.

Upgrade Fever. Open cabin doors. Prizes add up, upgrade keys move you from Inside to Oceanview, Balcony and Suite. Your final grade multiplies everything, up to x4. Three bad cabins and you check out.

Sunshine Voyage. Six ports, one free spin each. The forecast sets the multiplier: rainbow x5, sunshine x3, cloud x2, rain x1. A storm cancels the port. Every wild that lands is held for the rest of the voyage.

Summit Rush. Climb a shore trail for bigger prizes. Head back any time. A tuk-tuk can skip you a stop for free. Push too far and you miss All Aboard and lose the lot.

Cabin jackpots
