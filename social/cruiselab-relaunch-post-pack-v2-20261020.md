1. **3 second retention** (did the hook hold)
2. **Saves**
3. **Shares**

Saves and shares are the growth currency. Likes are noise. After the first full cycle: which pillar got the most saves and shares, and which platform sent the most bio-link clicks. Make more of the winner, drop the weakest.
